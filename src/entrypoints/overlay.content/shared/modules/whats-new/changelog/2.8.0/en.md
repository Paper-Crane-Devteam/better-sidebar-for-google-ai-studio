## ✂️ Save the Best for Last: Snippets

Ever had a 50-turn conversation with Gemini, but all you *really* wanted to keep was that one brilliant paragraph or that single flawless code block?

## 📚 A Buttery-Smooth, Revamped Sidebar

Over the past six weeks, I completely rebuilt the Sidebar UI from the ground up. It’s cleaner, highly organized, and most importantly, I've massively optimized the performance to **reduce animation lag**. It feels incredibly smooth now.

I also added a bunch of quality-of-life (and OCD-friendly) improvements:
*   **👀 Conversation Outline:** Chat getting too long? Pop open the new Outline view to instantly see the structure of your conversation.
*   **📝 Chat Remarks:** You can now add custom descriptions/notes to individual chats. 
*   **✨ Rich Tooltips:** Hover over any chat in the sidebar, and a beautiful tooltip will reveal its creation date, your custom description, and its tags.
*   **📌 Folder Upgrades:** You can finally **Pin** your favorite folders to the top! You can also **Drag & Drop** to reorder them, or use the new "Move to..." right-click option. Organization has never been this easy.
*   **🎯 "Locate Active Chat":** Lost in a sea of nested folders? Just click the new "Locate" button, and the tree will automatically expand and snap right to your current active conversation.

## ⚡ Workflow Boosters

*   **Default Homes for Gems & Notebooks:** Use Gems or Notebooks often? You can now assign a specific default folder to each Gem or Notebook. Any new chat created will automatically tuck itself into the right folder. No more manual sorting!
*   **Magic Slash `/` Commands:** Inside Gemini, simply type `/` in the input box to instantly summon your Prompt Library. Your workflow is about to get a whole lot faster.
*   **Batch Export:** Need a backup? You can now select multiple conversations and export them all at once.
