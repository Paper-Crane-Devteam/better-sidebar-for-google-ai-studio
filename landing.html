Questions & Answers

Does the free version stay free?
Yes. All core features — folders, tags, search, prompts, Drive sync, export — remain 100% free forever. Power Pack is purely for power users who want AI automation and premium customization.
How do I activate after purchase?
You'll receive a license key instantly. Open Better Sidebar → Settings → Packs → paste your key → click Activate. Takes 10 seconds.
What if I hit the device limit?
You can deactivate any device from Settings → Packs → Deactivate, then activate your new device. No support ticket needed.
Is my data safe?
100% local. We use an embedded SQLite database that runs entirely in your browser. No data is ever sent to our servers. The AI agent uses your own Gemini session — we never see your conversations.
Does the AI agent work on AI Studio too?
The AI Agent is currently Gemini-only. Premium themes and advanced export work on both Gemini and AI Studio.
What can the AI Agent actually do?
It has full query access to your extension's database — conversations, messages, folders, tags, prompts, snippets. It writes its own queries, analyzes results, and takes action. Built-in Skills include Auto-Organize, Backfill Search Index, Query Your Data, and Manage Prompts. You can also write your own custom Skills.
Does it use my API keys or tokens?
No. The Agent uses your existing Gemini session — no API keys, no extra tokens, no data leaving your machine. Everything runs locally in your browser.
