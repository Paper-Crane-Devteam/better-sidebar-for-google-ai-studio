【Additional Details】

What is Better Sidebar?
A browser extension (Chrome/Firefox) that transforms the default sidebar on Google Gemini and AI Studio into a full-featured productivity hub — folders, tags, full-text search, prompt library, Gem management, Google Drive sync, and more. 50,000+ weekly active users.

How does activation work?
After purchase, you'll receive a license key. Open Better Sidebar → Settings → Packs → enter your key. Activates instantly. Works on up to 3 devices.

What's the difference between Support Pack and Power Pack?
• Support Pack = Premium themes only
• Power Pack = Everything in Support Pack + AI Agent + Write Access + Full History + Advanced Export

If you already own a Support Pack, your key still works — just purchase the Power Pack for the upgrade.

What platforms are supported?
The AI Agent feature currently works on gemini.google.com. Theme features work on both Gemini and AI Studio. ChatGPT/Claude support is on the roadmap.

Refund policy?
Not satisfied? Full refund within 30 days, no questions asked.

常见问题

Better Sidebar 是什么？
一款浏览器扩展（Chrome/Firefox），将 Google Gemini 和 AI Studio 的默认侧边栏变成全功能生产力工具——文件夹、标签、全文搜索、Prompt 库、Gem 管理、Google Drive 同步等。50,000+ 周活用户。

购买后你会收到激活码。打开 Better Sidebar → 设置 → Packs → 输入激活码，即时生效。支持最多 3 台设备。
